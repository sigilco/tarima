## 2. Competitors / adjacent players

### Venue marketplaces (lend your venue to organizers)
- **Peerspace** — marketplace for hourly event/production space. Hosts list free; **20% host service fee** per booking; payout within 7 days. Raised ~$34–35M (last: $16M Series B led by GV, 2018); est. ~$100M revenue, ~2.3M monthly site visits.
  https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/10119442
  https://www.peerspace.com/blog/peerspace-series-b/
  https://linkedin.com/company/peerspace
- **Tagvenue** — venue marketplace (US/UK/AU/CA/SG). Free listing; commission **10% (US/CA/SG invoice), 12.5% (UK/IE/AU), 15% (online payment categories: meetings, production, dry hire)**. Claims 700k+ organizers.
  https://www.tagvenue.com/page/list-your-venue
  https://support.tagvenue.com/hc/en-gb/articles/32748248265757
- **Giggster** — locations for filming/photo/events. **19% host commission** + renter processing fee; free listing.
  https://help.giggster.com/en/articles/2832062
- **Splacer** — "Airbnb for event spaces" (Tel Aviv/NY); raised ~$8.7M (Viola, NFX); largely inactive post-2017.
  https://techcrunch.com/2015/07/01/splacer-with-1-4m-in-new-funding-is-airbnb-for-event-spaces/
  https://seedtable.com/companies/splacer
- **Eventup** — venue discovery marketplace; raised ~$8.5M; acquired by Gather→GrubHub; shut down Jan 2023.
  https://unicornburn.com/autopsy/eventup-venue-booking-usa
- **VenueBook** — digital event booking for restaurants; acquired by Seated (Aug 2020, part of Seated's $30M round); 120k planners; private events cited as ~15% of restaurant revenue and ~20% of profits.
  https://www.insightpartners.com/ideas/seated-announces-30m-in-funding-and-the-acquisition-of-venuebook/
- **Eventective** — US venue directory + lead market. Free profile; subscriptions from **$57/mo**; zero booking commission; pay-per-lead.
  https://www.eventective.com/pros/plans-pricing
- **HeadBox** — UK B2B meetings/events platform w/ AI tools; ~£16M+ raised (£3M round Apr 2025 led by Salica); expanding to NYC, claims $25M event enquiries there.
  https://www.conference-news.co.uk/news/headbox-secures-3m-investment-for-global-expansion-including-launch-in-nyc/
- **Spacebase** — Berlin meeting/workshop space booking (~20k spaces); ~$1.1M raised (2015); pivoted toward corporate lease/booking tooling.
  https://linkedin.com/company/spacebase
- **Appear Here** — short-term retail/pop-up marketplace; 10–30% of rent as fee; ~$21.4M raised (Balderton, Octopus).
  https://techcrunch.com/2017/05/17/appear-here-a-marketplace-for-short-term-retail-space-raises-12m-series-b/
  https://therealdeal.com/new-york/2017/04/19/uk-startup-wants-to-profit-from-new-yorks-retail-vacancy-epidemic/
- **Storefront (thestorefront.com)** — pop-up retail marketplace; $1.6M seed (2013, AngelPad); monetized via referral fees on ancillary purchases, not rent commission.
  https://techcrunch.com/2013/06/26/storefront-seed-funding/

### Event-organizer side (where venue "supply" meets organizers)
- **Luma** — free unlimited events; 5% platform fee on paid tickets; **Luma Plus $59/mo (annual)** removes fee; +$12/mo per extra admin; Stripe ~2.9%+30¢ separate.
  https://luma.com/pricing , https://help.luma.com/p/luma-plus
- **Meetup** — organizer subs: Standard from **$29.99/mo** ($174.99/yr, ≤3 groups); Pro from **$55/group/mo**; no free organizer tier since 2019.
  https://kannect.co/blog/compare/meetup-alternatives/ (citing Meetup help pages, Sep 2026)
  https://whos-in.app/blog/meetup-pricing-guide-2026
- **Eventbrite** — free for free events; **3.7% + $1.79/ticket + 2.9% processing** on paid tickets; optional Pro $15–100/mo. Acquired by Bending Spoons, closed Mar 2026, taken private.
  https://www.eventbrite.com/organizer/pricing/
  https://www.tickpick.com/blog/organizer/eventbrite-2026-fee-changes
- **Posh** — event ticketing ("TikTok for events"); $31M raised (Series A $22M Goodwater, 2024); organizer free; attendees pay **10% + $0.99**.
  https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/23/event-startup-posh-raises-22m-in-to-focus-on-personalisation-and-event-diversification/
- **Partiful** — RSVP app; ~$27M raised, $140M val (a16z, 2022). Freemium; launched native ticketing Jun 2026 at **3.5% + $0.30** via Stripe; ~2.3M MAU.
  https://sacra.com/c/partiful/
  https://networkherald.com/2026/06/05/partiful-launches-built-in-ticket-payments-what-changes-for-organizers/
- **Bookwhen** — class/event booking SaaS: free tier (50 bookings/mo), Lite $16/mo, Standard $29/mo, Plus $59/mo.
  https://bookwhen.com/pricing

## 3. Pricing patterns for comparable B2B SMB platforms (2026)

| Model | Examples |
|---|---|
| Flat monthly per-location tiers | Perfect Venue $119–339/mo/location; Tock $79–899/mo; Resy $249–399/mo; Bookwhen $16–59/mo; Eventective from $57/mo; Meetup $29.99/mo + $55/group Pro |
| Commission/take-rate on bookings | Peerspace 20% host; Giggster 19%; Tagvenue 10–15%; Appear Here 10–30% of rent; Posh 10%+$0.99 attendee; Luma 5% free plan |
| Per-ticket/per-cover fees | Eventbrite 3.7%+$1.79+2.9%; OpenTable $1.00–1.50/network cover; Partiful 3.5%+$0.30; Tock 2–3% prepayment fee |
| Freemium → subscription | Luma (free→$59/mo), Bookwhen (free→$16+), Perfect Venue (free→$119+), Eventective (free→$57+) |
| Hybrid subscription + processing | Perfect Venue 2.8–4.8% processing; Tock 2–3% prepayments; OpenTable subs + covers |
| Sales-led/quote pricing | SevenRooms, Tripleseat (~$350–650/mo/venue + ~$1.5k onboarding) |
| Multi-location pricing | Per-location/per-venue is the norm: Perfect Venue "/location", Tripleseat "/venue" + volume scaling, Meetup Pro $55/**group**, Luma Plus per **calendar** |
| Marketplace listing (free) + commission | Peerspace, Tagvenue, Giggster, Appear Here — all free to list, monetize per booking |
| Marketing ad-spend model | Yelp Ads from $150/mo ($5/day); Toast Advertising from $15/day |

  https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/SEPDF/cache/10092.pdf
  https://datassential.com/resource/mapping-the-european-foodservice-market-e200-billion-in-opportunity/

- **SMB software spend:** median SMB software spend ~$287/mo (8.7 tools); micro (1–10 emp) ~$124/mo; marketing/CRM largest category at ~$69/mo avg (~24% of budget).
  https://mewayz.cloud/en/blog/smb-software-spend-report-2026-what-138000-businesses-actually-pay-for-tools
