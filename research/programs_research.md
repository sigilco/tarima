## Notes
- **Actionable before year-end for a Barcelona founder:** YC W27 (Nov 2), PearX W27 (Oct 4), Techstars Spring 2027 (Nov 18), 500 Global B36 (Oct 11), speedrun SR008 priority window (Oct 12–Nov 1), Founders Inc Artifact (Dec 31), Antler rolling, EF Spring 2027 interest, Seedcamp/Kima rolling.
- **Geo constraints:** Neo Residency is North-America-only. a16z speedrun requires in-person SF. Antler/EF/Station F/Lanzadera all require EU/ES relocation or presence — Antler (Berlin) and Lanzadera (Valencia) are the closest.
- **Sector fit:** Marketplace + SMB SaaS is squarely in YC/Techstars/Pear/500 Global territory; speedrun skews consumer/AI; SPC and HF0 skew deep-tech/repeat-founder; Wayra/Plug and Play need strategic/vertical fit.
