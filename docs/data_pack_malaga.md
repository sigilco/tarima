# Málaga Data Pack — Public/Community Events Market

For: off-peak booking layer for independent venues (organiser-pays fee; venue keeps 100% F&B).
Compiled: 2026-10-04. All platform data sampled same day unless noted.

## 0. Confidence key and overall method

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **COUNTED** | Directly observed on the source (attendee counts, member counts, published schedules). |
| **SAMPLED** | Extrapolated from an enumerated but non-exhaustive sample of events/venues. |
| **ESTIMATED** | Informed estimate from descriptive evidence (e.g. "hundreds attend weekly"), flagged wherever used. |

**Method.** Platform sweep on 2026-10-04: Meetup `find` events+groups pages for Málaga (live listing, not a census — first pages enumerate ~17 groups and ~12 upcoming events before truncation; extended by targeted per-group search), Eventbrite Málaga discovery page, Couchsurfing Málaga location page, InterNations Málaga public pages, mundolingo.org, polyglotclub.com, Xceed, organiser/venue websites, and Málaga press agendas (malagahoy.es, laopiniondemalaga.es). Facebook groups referenced by name+member count only — Facebook event counts are not countable without login (stated, not guessed). Nothing below presents an estimate as a count.

**Headline honesty check:** Málaga is a thin-but-real market. The recurring community-events layer is dominated by language exchanges and expat/nomad social formats; tech/professional meetups are small and mostly monthly; the "organised dinner/workshop" layer is very thin. That said, the single most important discovery is that **the product's ideal venue already exists and is observable**: Bakkano 2.0 (C/ Hernán Ruiz 13, Centro) self-programs a different community event every Mon–Thu night and closes Sunday. The model is not hypothetical in Málaga — it is under-aggregated.

Huelin is also where nomads/expats actually live — cheap rents, big flats — so venues have local, repeat, non-tourist demand. Soho is the honest runner-up and overlaps Centro operationally. Teatinos is student-dense but venue-poor (Play Planet is the exception that proves it: a board-game café built for exactly this use). El Palo/Pedregalejo is seasonal-beach and low-frequency. Province cities are a different playbook (expat retirees, English-pub formats) — note but don't mix.

## 3. Temporal distribution — day-of-week × start hour

**Basis:** ~35 dated recurring/upcoming events enumerated across Meetup, Couchsurfing, Eventbrite, venue calendars and organiser pages (SAMPLED). Spanish-evening reality confirmed: community events start 19:00–22:30; professional networking starts 17:30–19:00.

| Day | Recurring events observed (sample) | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Bakkano Beer Pong 22:00; Torremolinos Lunch Club (lunch); InterNations occasionally Mon 20:00; salsa schools (Báilame/Muévete Mon–Thu classes) | ~3 + dance classes |
| Tue | Mundo Lingo 21:30 (Bakkano); Boardgame Night 17:30 (Play Planet); Tuesday Pub Quiz 18:00; Tuesday Trivial Night 19:30; Báilame El Palo 18–22:00 | ~5 |
| Wed | Bakkano Intl Party 22:00; El Colmao Intl Meetup 19:15; PowerTalks Toastmasters 19:00; Malaga Style bachata outdoor 20:30 (Huelin); InterNations official events often Wed 20:00; salsa classes | ~6 |
| **Thu** | Málaga Tándem 21:00 (Sala Premier); Mundo Lingo Thu edition (O'Hara) + Huelin edition 19:00; Malaga-AI 18:30; She WINs ~18:30; Malbec&Vinyl after-office 17:30; Spirit yoga Parque Huelin 19:00; Amans open mic 21:00 (La Llave); Museum Pub quiz 21:00; Bakkano Game Zone 22:00; Torremolinos Intl 21:00; PolyglotClub quiz 20:00 (Bartola); salsa classes | **~10–12 — peak weeknight** |
| Fri | Tándem Fun Fridays 21:00 (Garrison's); ANTOJO "Fast Pass" 19:00; CS Fridays Fuengirola; Muévete social 23:30; Recyclo live music | ~5 |
| Sat | BIZNART, one-off cultural/commercial events, salsa socials, hikes | ~5 (but mostly one-offs) |
| Sun | Pachange Speed Friending 19:00; MalagaMeet beach volleyball 16:00 + CS karaoke 21:00; Third Place improv JAM 16:00; Bakkano **closed** | ~4 |

**Hour distribution (sampled evening events):** 17:30–18:59 ≈ 20% (professional/networking formats); 19:00–20:29 ≈ 40%; 20:30–22:00 ≈ 30%; after 22:00 ≈ 10% (bar-programmed socials).

**So-what:** Organiser demand already sits inside venue dead hours — Tue/Wed/Thu 19:00–22:30 is the densest window, and **Thursday is the single busiest community-event night**, not Friday. This is the friendliest possible structure for the pitch: venues aren't being asked to surrender prime hours, and organisers aren't being asked to move off nights that already work. Bakkano closing Sunday and programming Mon–Thu is the market's own proof of the dead-hours logic.

## 4. Venue types used today

**Basis:** venue types for the identified recurring-event set (SAMPLED).

| Venue type | Share (sampled) | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Independent bars / pubs / cafés | **~45–55%** | Bakkano 2.0, Sala Premier, El Colmao, Garrison's, O'Hara, Museum, Camden Lock, Bartola, Le Grand, La Madriguera, Recyclo, Play Planet |
| Dedicated activity rooms (dance studios, game cafés, cultural centres) | ~20% | Espacio Bohemia, La Llave, Báilame studios, Muévete |
| Coworking / innovation spaces | ~15% | Innovation Campus (Malaga-AI, PyData, Cursor, Job Fair), TLR, Impact Hub, Niage Garage, La Farola |
| Public space (parks, beach) | ~10% | Parque Huelin yoga, Playa Huelin bachata/volleyball, hikes |
| Hotels / institutional / other | ~5% | ME Málaga, Hermandad del Sepulcro (InterNations), Rayuela bookshop |

**So-what:** Independent bars/cafés already host roughly half of the recurring layer — supply-side education is partial, not zero. The dance studios and coworking spaces are the "incumbents" we partially displace; the gap is that bar-hosted formats are informal (verbal deals, WhatsApp) with no structure, deposit, or portability — exactly the transaction the product formalises.

| 150+ | Festivals/conventions (Comic-Con, Sun&Tech) — out of scope | ~5% |

**So-what:** The product's capacity sweet spot is **15–60 people** — which matches a bar's private room or a café's full evening buyout, not a function hall. ## 9. The honest "how thin is thin" assessment

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Weekly venue-hosted community events in Málaga city (all formats, recurring) | ~35–55/week (SAMPLED→ESTIMATED: ~35 identified by name + dance-class volume; true figure likely 40–60 incl. informal/unlisted) |
| …of which Mon–Thu evening | **~20–30/week** — the target band |
| Distinct recurring organisers identifiable | ~31 named (this pack); est. 40–60 total incl. Facebook-only groups |
| Events/week on Meetup alone | ~15–25 (SAMPLED listing pages) |
| Events/month on Couchsurfing | 140 claimed by platform — real booked-venue subset est. 15–30 |
| Versus SF | Probably **1/10 to 1/20** the organised meetup density per capita — thin, but concentrated in formats (language/social) that are *more* venue-friendly than SF's tech talks |
| Net verdict | Viable for a manual-concierge POC (60 organiser contacts is realistic; there are ~30 warm targets in this doc alone). Thin means the platform story ("agent-callable supply") outruns the demand story here — Málaga answers "does the model work where we hand-create both sides," not "is there latent demand to harvest." |

**Two structural findings that matter more than the counts:**
1. **Proof-of-model venue exists.** Bakkano 2.0 already runs Mon Beer Pong / Tue Mundo Lingo / Wed Intl Party / Thu Game Zone and closes Sunday. This is the entire thesis running in one bar. Study it, price against it, cite it in the YC application.
2. **Thursday is Málaga's community night** (≥10 recurring events) and **the €3 cover / 1-drink-minimum informal contract is the de-facto price** of a hosted slot today. That anchors fee-testing: organisers already pay €3/head to attend someone else's hosted event — a €25 flat per booking for a guaranteed slot is not a hard sell against that baseline; €50 probably is for weekly formats (worth ~€150–250/month — calibrate the ladder accordingly).
