You are TWO adversarial characters reviewing a startup plan in sequence. Read the attached scope doc + POC draft first.

The plan: recurring event organisers book independent venues' dead hours through the platform; organiser pays $/€20–75 per booking or % of tickets; venue pays 0 and keeps 100% of F&B. POC: 40 venue walk-ins + 60 organiser DMs per city (SF + Málaga), Oct 6–24.

CHARACTER 1 — the venue owner (do this twice: a Mission-district SF café/bar owner who gets pitched weekly, and a Málaga centro bar/café owner who answers WhatsApp when she feels like it):

- Do I even have "dead hours" I'd monetize, or do I close early/restock/clean then? Is "empty Tuesday" actually inventory?
- What makes me NOT take the organiser's next booking direct and cut you out?

CHARACTER 2 — the recurring organiser (SF tech-meetup host with 80 attendees/mo; Málaga language-exchange host with 25):
- Why pay a booking fee when I can DM cafés myself for free? What exactly am I buying — certainty, speed, min-spend deals I can't negotiate alone, legal cover?
- Is a café/bar actually the venue I want, or do I need AV/quiet/seating a restaurant can't give?
- Your fee vs my alternatives: Luma handles my RSVP for free/5%; Meetup takes my $30/mo; why does the VENUE booking need a fee on top?
- Recurring means I lock a venue for months — so you get ONE fee from me then I'm disintermediated. How is that a business?
