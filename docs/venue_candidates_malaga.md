## Adjacent infrastructure (not F&B candidates — useful anchors)

- **The Living Room (TLR Coworking)**, C/ Barroso 15, Soho — 150-member international community with weekly/monthly events; better as an *organiser channel* than a venue (their members need external venues).
- **Innovation Campus** (Centro + Malaga Terrace, C/ Puerto 14) — hosts Malaga-AI, PyData, Cursor, Job Fair; watch who they turn away (they close 18:30 weekdays → evening events are literally outside their hours — a referral channel, not a venue).
- **Impact Hub Málaga** (El Perchel) — event space, hosts ticketed cultural events.
- **La Casa Invisible** (C/ Andrés Pérez 8) — self-managed social centre; programming partner for arts/talks, not commercial supply.
- **Rayuela bookshop** — 25-year monthly tertulia; cultural anchor, not F&B.

## Notes for the walk-in plan

- **One walk covers Tier 1+2 Centro**: Hernán Ruiz (Bakkano, Bartola) → Molina Lario (Sala Premier) → Comedias (Garrison's) → Convalecientes (Museum, Camden Lock) → Carretería (La Madriguera, Kima) → Plaza Camas/García-Herrera (Recyclo, Santa Coffee). ~90 min on foot.
- **Owner-present hours in ES**: 16:00–18:00 (post-lunch lull) for cafés/bars — matches POC plan's 15–17h.
- **Anchor pitch number**: Thursday is the busiest community night (~10 recurring events citywide); the de-facto hosted-slot price today is €3/head or a drink minimum — leads with "we fill your other three dead nights."
- La Tetería (C/ San Agustín 9) — famous tea house, perfect archetype — **closing Dec 2025** (Diario Sur); exclude unless it relocates.
