# Data → pitch mapping (who sees which numbers)

Same data pack, three audiences, three different weapons.

## To the venue owner (walk-in / one-pager)

The goal: prove demand exists *on their street, on their dead nights* — not "the events market is big".

- **Neighborhood event density** → "{N} public events ran within 800m of your door in the last 90 days. {X} of them were in venues like yours."
- **Day×hour overlap map** → "Your dead window (Tue–Wed 19–22h) overlaps with {Y} events/week of demand in {city}."
- **Group-size × spend math** → "A 25-person meetup is ~{€/$ amount} on your till at typical spend. Your normal Tuesday does ~{baseline}."
- **Named formats nearby** → "3 book clubs and a language exchange already run monthly in {neighborhood} — none have a standing venue."
- What NOT to show: TAM slides, platform features, the word "AI".

## To the organiser (DM / at-event pitch)

The goal: prove we remove their actual pain — venue hunting is the #1 recurring time cost.

- **Slot inventory** → "We hold {N} verified slots on {days} in {neighborhoods} — verified means the owner stated terms, not 'open till late'."
- **Their alternatives' cost** → "Peerspace averages ${hourly}/hr for a meetup room; our min-spend deals cost you $0 + attendees buy drinks."
- **Reliability** → "Deposit-backed bookings: the room is confirmed or you don't lose the evening. Your Tuesday meetup has a home every week, not a new negotiation."
- **One-message booking** → the demo: send them the venue card, they reply "yes", done.

## To the accelerator (the application's numbers box)

- **Behavioral funnel counts** — events run, rebookings, fees collected, till lifts (the investor-approved metrics).
- **The data pack itself as evidence of founder speed** — "we mapped both cities' off-peak event inventory in a week" is a speed signal; the pack becomes an appendix.
- **Supply-index uniqueness** — "the only terms-level index of off-peak independent-venue inventory" + per-city supply-acquisition cost as a measured number.
- **Market context:** private/hosted events ~15% of restaurant revenue / ~20% of profits (VenueBook/Seated); experiential bookings +46% YoY (OpenTable); graveyard acknowledged + named — "Splacer/Eventup died selling TO venues at low frequency; we sell FOR venues and charge the side with recurring need — with a recurring money flow, which is what they lacked."

## The anti-pitch (what kills credibility)

- "Events market is huge" — hand-wavy; every partner knows the graveyard.
- Claiming the falsification pair (two cities) isolates a variable — it doesn't; say "portability datapoint" instead.
- "Agent-callable" without the endpoint running — build the read-only JSON endpoint week 2 so the claim is literally true.
- Soft-yeses presented as validation — partners read "15 venues said yes" as "15 venues were polite".
