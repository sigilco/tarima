The core pitch stat: one in five SF Meetup events lists no venue — organisers publicly post events without a home. In Málaga the behavior already exists informally: Bakkano 2.0 self-programs Mon beer-pong / Tue Mundo Lingo / Wed intl party / Thu game-zone and closes Sunday — the entire thesis running in one bar.

City data

Neighborhood share of events — Mission is #1

Mission also carries 25% of all Mon–Thu evening events (66/260). FiDi/SoMa leads ticketed platforms but its venue economics are wrong for walk-ins (corporate, chains, lunch-rush). POC pick: Mission + Hayes Valley/Divisadero (Marina alternate).

Day-of-week — Mon–Thu is 46% of listings

Evening starts cluster at 18:00 (134) and 19:00 (144). Wed/Thu are the strongest weeknights. ~60 listed events run on a dead Tuesday in SF — the pitch sentence for venue walk-ins.

The café gap is supply-side, not demand: cafés are absent from evening events because none are open — exactly the dead-hour inventory the product unlocks.

Centro ≈15 named venues on one walkable grid; Huelin is where nomads/expats actually live (beats the Soho hypothesis); Soho folds into Centro outreach; Teatinos is student-dense but venue-poor; El Palo is seasonal.

Timing — Thursday is the community night

Sampled recurring events: Thu ~10–12 (peak), Wed ~6, Tue ~5, Mon ~3, Fri ~5, Sun ~4. Start hours: 19:00–20:29 ≈40%, 20:30–22:00 ≈30%, 17:30–18:59 ≈20% (professional formats), >22:00 ≈10%. The Tue/Wed/Thu 19:00–22:30 window is where organiser demand meets dead venue hours.

The price anchor that exists today

The de-facto hosted-slot price is €3/head cover or a one-drink minimum (Tándem charges €3 at Sala Premier; Mundo Lingo runs free with the venue keeping the bar). Organisers already pay to attend — a €20–25 flat fee per guaranteed booking is an easy sell against that; €50 needs min-spend framing.

Málaga POC call: Centro Histórico (90-min walk covers 14 warm venues — Bakkano, Sala Premier, Garrison's, El Colmao, O'Hara, Bartola, Museum Pub) + Huelin second. 31 named organisers; Meetup + InterNations + Couchsurfing + Telegram are the channels (Luma ≈ zero presence here). Honest size: ~1/10–1/20 SF density — viable for concierge POC, thin for the platform story.

Caveats & method
  • SF window is forward listings Oct 4–Nov 4 (public sources don't enumerate history); recurrence inferred from series flags and multi-event groups. SF Tech Week Oct 5–12 inflates tech/founder counts ±2–3pts.
  • Meetup RSVPs undercount actual attendance ~1.5–3×; median listed RSVP is 2 but community-format mass sits at 5–35.
  • Eventbrite: 4,293 total counted, 499 sampled for detail (category shares are sampled).
  • Málaga is sampled/estimated, not counted — Facebook groups (Expats in Málaga 106K members) can't be enumerated without login; figures stated as such.
