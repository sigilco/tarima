The entry screen of the two-step OTP login. Users arrive from the header
sign-in link or are redirected here by route guards. The step lives in the
URL (`?step=email|code&email=…`) so a refresh lands on the same step; the
component holds email/code/busy/error in local state. POST /auth/request-otp
sends the code (beta allowlist enforced server-side). Off-white #FAFAFA
surface, ink foreground, clay #b8491f primary button; amber #FDE047 only as
a small accent mark. Same family as the landing hero, calmer.
