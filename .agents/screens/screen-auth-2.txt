Second step of OTP login, reachable only with `email` in the URL
(beforeLoad redirects to step=email otherwise). Shows the 6-digit code
input, a resend link, a change-email link, and a client-side expiry
countdown (codes expire server-side; the countdown is a hint only).
POST /auth/verify-otp sets the `tarima_session` cookie on success and the
route redirects to /app.

Transitions: Verify → 200 → navigate /app (replace, cookie set by the
API); Verify → 401/403 → inline danger copy, stays on step; Resend →
request-otp 200 → countdown restarts; Use another email → step=email.
Animations: countdown ticks once per second (text swap, no motion);
otherwise shadcn defaults only.
